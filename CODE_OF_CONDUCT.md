# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MELvan contributors and maintainers commit to providing a welcoming,
harassment-free community for everyone. Be respectful, constructive, and
considerate of different experiences and perspectives.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and disagreements with professionalism and empathy.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not disclose personal or program data.
- Focus criticism on the work, never on the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and make reasonable efforts to repair harm.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `mills.martin@gmail.com`. Do not open a
public issue containing sensitive details. Reports will be reviewed fairly and
confidentially to the extent practicable. Maintainers may remove content,
restrict participation temporarily, or permanently exclude a participant when
necessary to protect the community.

Anyone handling a report must disclose conflicts of interest and recuse
themselves where impartial review is not possible. Retaliation against a person
who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project. It complements, and does not replace, applicable law or platform
terms.
