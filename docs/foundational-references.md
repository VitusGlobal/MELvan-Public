# MELvan Foundational References

Status: canonical product rationale, version `1.0.0`.

MELvan derives its internal logic from the following foundational references. They are canonical to MELvan, including public distributions and forks; they are not optional grounding materials chosen independently by each operator.

## Canonical sources

| Source | Official reference | Role in MELvan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Global Affairs Canada, *Results-Based Management for International Assistance Programming: A How-to Guide* | [Official guide](https://www.international.gc.ca/world-monde/funding-financement/results_based_management-gestion_axee_resultats-guide.aspx?lang=eng) | Governs MELvan's initial Results-Based Management semantics, including Activity, Output, Outcome, Indicator, result formulation, and neutral measurable wording. |
| W.K. Kellogg Foundation, *Logic Model Development Guide* | [Foundation resource](https://wkkf.issuelab.org/resource/logic-model-development-guide.html) | Complements the RBM guide with logic-model structure, causal-chain reasoning, assumptions, context, and worked examples. |

[RECAP](https://www.vitushq.com/en/blog/tired-of-vague-advice-on-writing-a-theory-of-change-narrative-try-the-recap-framework/) is MELvan's canonical structure for capturing Risks, Evidence, Context, Assumptions, and People around a Logic Model.

The absence of a source binary from a public repository for copyright or redistribution reasons does not make that source optional. The official links preserve access to the canonical material.

## Authority

1. An explicit Reviewer correction is authoritative for the current Statement or Evidence assessment.
2. The active program Logic Model and Performance Measurement Framework (PMF) are authoritative for program relevance, terminology, and mapping to existing program elements.
3. The canonical foundational references govern MELvan's general RBM interpretation, classification, formulation, indicator construction, and causal-consistency reasoning.
4. Model knowledge may assist within those boundaries but may not override them.

Within the foundational references, the Global Affairs Canada guide governs the initial RBM classification and formulation conventions. The Kellogg guide complements it with broader logic-model construction and causal-chain guidance. MELvan must not silently blend incompatible definitions: a governed product decision, classifier rule, and regression example must resolve any material conflict.

## How MELvan uses the references

The references inform MELvan's architecture and principles. Their logic is embodied in versioned classifier rules, prompts, schemas, validation, workflows, and tests. MELvan does not require a separate runtime context pack, and the source documents do not need to be sent to the model with each request.

Program-specific Logic Models and PMFs remain separate from the canonical product references. Reviewer feedback may correct a program assessment and provide governed learning evidence, but it cannot silently change the canonical foundations or global product rules.

An operator may add supplementary foundational material for its domain or program. Additions must retain source, license, version, and authority metadata and may not remove, replace, weaken, or override the canonical references without an explicit governed MELvan product change.

## Public distribution

Public distributions include this document and the official source links. They bundle third-party source files only when redistribution permission has been verified. Public and private distributions have the same purpose, principles, authority hierarchy, RECAP model, and health-domain safeguards; public packaging differs only by excluding sensitive data, deployment-specific values, unlicensed binaries, and development ephemera.
