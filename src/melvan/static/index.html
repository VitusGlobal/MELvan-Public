MELvan User Guide

MELVan is an assistant that helps teams capture, classify, and make sense of emerging results using their existing program theory or logic model.

Do use MELVan if you:

  • Already have a program theory or logic model.
  • Understand basic Results-Based Management concepts, including outcomes, outputs, and indicators.
  • Want to capture emerging results, including positive, negative, and unexpected changes.
  • Can provide relevant program context, including your program description, logic model or theory of change, people and teams involved, and performance measurement framework.
  • Have a process to periodically review MELVan’s log for learning.
  • Can engage someone with program evaluation expertise in sense-making.

Don’t use MELVan if you:

  • Do not yet have a program theory or logic model.
  • Need an introduction to Results-Based Management or program evaluation.
  • Cannot provide relevant program context, including your program description, logic model or theory of change, people and teams involved, and performance measurement framework.
  • Do not have a plan to review and validate what MELVan captures.
  • Want MELVan to replace evaluative thinking. It won’t work.
